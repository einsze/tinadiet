## 1. Who we are

Tina Diet is a personal AI nutrition coach service delivered through a LINE Official Account ("OA") and a LINE Front-end Framework ("LIFF") web application. This policy describes how we collect, use, share, store, and protect personal data when you use the service.

In this policy:

- **"We"**, **"us"**, **"our"** mean the operator of Tina Diet.
- **"You"**, **"your"** mean the individual using Tina Diet.
- **"Service"** means the Tina Diet LINE OA, LIFF app, and supporting backend.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide directly

- **LINE profile information** — your LINE user ID and (if you grant permission) your display name. Provided automatically when you add the LINE OA or open the LIFF app.
- **Nutrition profile** — gender, date of birth, height, current weight, target weight, activity level, and goal type (loss / maintain / gain).
- **Food logs** — descriptions and photos of meals you send to the LINE OA, and any food entries you create manually in the LIFF app.
- **Weight logs** — weight readings you submit via chat or LIFF.
- **Chat messages** — questions you ask the consultation feature, and Tina's responses.
- **Payment information** — if you subscribe to Premium, your payment details are collected and processed directly by our payment processor(s) (currently Stripe; additional Thai-friendly providers such as PromptPay, TrueMoney, and LINE Pay may be added in the future). We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers — only a customer reference identifier and subscription status.

### 2.2 Data generated automatically

- **Derived nutrition data** — basal metabolic rate (BMR), total daily energy expenditure (TDEE), daily calorie and macro targets, streak counts, and weekly aggregates calculated from your inputs.
- **Service logs** — timestamps, request metadata, error logs, and aggregate usage counts (e.g. how many photos or consultation questions you used today) needed to operate the service, enforce quotas, debug issues, and prevent abuse.

### 2.3 Data we do **not** collect

- We do not access your phone contacts, location, calendar, files outside of what you send to the chat, or any other LINE messages outside of our OA.
- We do not collect data from users we have reason to believe are under the age of 13.

## 3. How we use your data

We use the data above to:

1. **Provide the core service** — parse food messages, calculate calories, generate dashboards, send daily and weekly summaries, deliver consultation answers, run the meal coach.
2. **Personalize advice** — Tina references your goals, recent intake, weight trend, and streak when generating consultation answers and coach suggestions.
3. **Process payments** — create and manage your Premium subscription with our payment processor(s).
4. **Communicate with you** — deliver push notifications (daily summaries, weekly summaries, coach suggestions, billing reminders).
5. **Improve and secure the service** — debug issues, monitor quotas, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve features in aggregate.

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do not show third-party advertising in the service.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the following legal bases under the PDPA and equivalent international frameworks:

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the service you signed up for (Article 24(3) PDPA equivalent).
- **Consent** — for optional features and any future marketing communications.
- **Legitimate interests** — to operate, secure, and improve the service.
- **Legal obligation** — to comply with tax, payment, and other regulatory requirements.

## 5. Sharing your data with third parties

We share data only with the third-party services strictly required to operate Tina Diet. Each service receives only the minimum data needed.

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OpenAI** (USA) | AI parsing of food text and photos, generating consultation answers and coach suggestions | The text or image of your message, plus relevant context (profile summary, recent logs, daily totals). No payment or LINE user ID. |
| **Stripe** (USA / global) | Payment processing for Premium subscription | Email or LINE user reference, subscription metadata, payment instrument details (handled directly by Stripe — never touched by our servers). |
| **LINE Corporation** (Japan) | Account login, message delivery, push notifications | LINE user ID, message content and timestamps necessary to deliver replies. |
| **Railway** (USA) | Backend application hosting | All operational data on encrypted infrastructure. |
| **Cloudflare** (USA / global) | LIFF web app hosting and CDN | Static web assets and request metadata. |

Additional payment processors (such as PromptPay, TrueMoney, LINE Pay) may be added in the future. If we add a new third party that materially changes how your data is processed, we will update this policy and notify you in-app.

We do not transfer your data to any other third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

## 6. International data transfers

Some of our processors are located outside Thailand. Where this is the case we rely on adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or the providers' published safeguards (such as Stripe's and OpenAI's published data processing terms). By using Tina Diet you acknowledge that your data may be processed outside Thailand.

## 7. Data retention

- **Active account data** — retained for as long as your account exists and you continue using the service.
- **Chat history** — retained indefinitely while your account is active so Tina can offer multi-turn coherence; you may request deletion at any time.
- **Payment records** — retained as required by tax and accounting law (typically up to 5 years after the relevant transaction, depending on jurisdiction).
- **Logs and aggregate analytics** — retained for up to 12 months for security, debugging, and quota enforcement.
- **After account deletion** — your personal data is hard-deleted from our database within 30 days of your deletion request, except payment records that we are legally required to keep.

## 8. Your rights under PDPA

Subject to local conditions and exceptions, you have the right to:

1. **Access** your personal data we hold about you.
2. **Receive a copy** of your data in a portable format (available in-app: Settings → Export My Data).
3. **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data (available in-app: Settings → Edit Profile).
4. **Delete** your account and data (available in-app: Settings → Delete Account).
5. **Restrict or object** to certain processing.
6. **Withdraw consent** for any consent-based processing.
7. **Lodge a complaint** with the Personal Data Protection Committee of Thailand (PDPC) or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any right not available in-app, contact us at the email below.

## 9. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures including:

- TLS encryption in transit between LINE, your device, and our servers.
- Encrypted storage on our hosting provider's volumes.
- Authenticated API access protected by short-lived session tokens.
- Quota and abuse-prevention systems.
- Webhook signature verification for payment events.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the appropriate authority as required by the PDPA.

## 10. Children

Tina Diet is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, contact us and we will delete it.
